📖 Key Metrics Explained
▼
ACV — Annual Contract Value
The total revenue one customer pays per year. Calculated as total annual revenue divided by number of customers.
Formula: Total ARR ÷ Customers
ARPA — Average Revenue Per Account
Essentially the same as ACV, but used in monthly terms by analysts and investors. Used to track pricing trends over time.
Formula: MRR ÷ Customers (monthly)
LTV — Lifetime Value
The total gross profit a customer generates over their entire relationship with you. Higher LTV means you can afford to spend more acquiring each customer.
Formula: (ACV × Gross Margin) ÷ Churn Rate
CAC — Customer Acquisition Cost
The total sales and marketing spend required to win one new customer. The ratio of LTV to CAC tells you whether your growth is sustainable.
Formula: Total S&M Spend ÷ New Customers Won
The key relationship: LTV should be at least 3× CAC for a healthy SaaS business. LTV:CAC below 3× means you're spending too much to acquire customers relative to what they're worth. Above 5× often means you're under-investing in growth.

📢 What is Go-to-Market (GTM)?
Your GTM strategy is how you acquire and convert customers.

How Go-to-Market (GTM) Changes by Animal
▼
🐭 Mice: Product-Led Growth (PLG) — Users self-serve, viral loops, freemium. No sales team needed.
🐰 Rabbits: Marketing-Led Growth (MLG) + Self-Serve — Marketing generates leads, light sales assistance.
🦌 Deer: MLG + Sales-Led Growth (SLG) — Marketing + inside sales team. Sales cycles 1–2 months.
🐘 Elephants: Sales-Led Growth (SLG) — Enterprise sales, field teams, ABM. Long cycles 1–2 quarters.
🐋 Whales: Founder-Led Sales — Founder involvement, strategic partnerships. Cycles 6–18 months.

💡 How to think about budget allocation
▼
👤 Personnel costs
All salaries, commissions and recruiting costs for people directly involved in winning new customers.

Includes: AE, SDR, BDR, Solutions Engineer (pre-sales portion), Sales Manager, recruitment fees.

Does not include: Customer Success (post-signing), Product, Engineering.
🤝 Sales & acquisition activities
Direct costs of moving a deal forward — everything that happens between first contact and signed contract.

Includes: Travel, demos, PoC/pilots, conferences, sales tools (CRM, Outreach, LinkedIn Sales Nav), entertainment.

Note: Travel and tools are per-person costs — they belong here, not in personnel.
📣 Marketing & demand generation
Everything that creates awareness and generates inbound leads before a salesperson is involved.

Includes: Paid ads, content, SEO/SEM, marketing automation (HubSpot), webinars, PR, brand, field marketing events.

Note: For Mice/Rabbits this is the primary bucket. For Elephants it is mostly ABM support.
Default splits are based on industry benchmarks for your animal type. You can adjust per year — for example higher personnel in Year 1 when you are hiring, shifting to more marketing in Year 2 as the team is ramped up.
